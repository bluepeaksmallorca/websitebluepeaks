Most people who walk with us book a private day. It is the simplest arrangement: your group, one of us, and a route we choose together.

## How it works

**You write to us** with your dates, how many of you there are, and an honest sense of what you are used to. "We walk a lot at home but nothing steep" is more useful than a fitness self-assessment.

**We suggest two or three routes.** Not a catalogue — two or three that actually fit what you have told us, with the real numbers attached so you can weigh them up.

**We agree a plan**, including where to meet, what time, and how you are getting there. If a route needs a car at one end or a taxi at the other, we sort that out in advance rather than on the morning.

**We walk.** And if the weather has other ideas, we adapt. Living here means we know which valley is sheltered when the tramuntana wind is up, and which north face holds ice into March.

## What a day looks like

A typical day is five to seven hours of walking with a proper stop for lunch. We start early — earlier than most people expect — because the light is better, the trails are empty, and in anything above about 18 °C it makes the climb far more pleasant.

We are not in a hurry. If there is something worth stopping for, we stop. If your group wants to swim at the end, we plan a route that finishes at water.

## Who this suits

Private days work well for:

- **Couples and families** who would rather not walk with strangers
- **Groups of friends** with mixed fitness, where the pace needs to flex
- **First-time visitors** who want an introduction to the range rather than a specific summit
- **Repeat visitors** who have done the obvious routes and want what comes after them

## What it costs

**€180 for the whole group**, up to eight people. That is the guiding fee for a full day. It does not change with group size, which is why it works out well for four or more.

Above eight people we bring a second guide and the price changes; write to us and we will quote it plainly.
