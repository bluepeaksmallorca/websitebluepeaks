We offer private group hikes all over Mallorca, tailored to your needs and with a mindful approach if you are open to this.

## How it works

**Tell us your dates**, how many of you there are, and an honest sense of what you are used to. "We walk a lot at home but nothing steep" is more useful than a fitness self-assessment.

**We suggest routes** that actually fit what you have told us — not a catalogue, two or three options with the real numbers attached.

**We agree a plan**, including where to meet, what time, and how you are getting there.

**We walk.** And if the weather has other ideas, we adapt. Living here means knowing which valley is sheltered when the tramuntana wind is up.

## What to expect

A typical day is five to seven hours of walking with a proper stop for lunch. We start early — earlier than most people expect — because the light is better, the trails are empty, and in anything above about 18 °C it makes the climb far more pleasant.

We are not in a hurry. If there is something worth stopping for, we stop.
