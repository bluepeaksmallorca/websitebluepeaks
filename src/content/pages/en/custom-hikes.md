Some people arrive knowing exactly what they want. A peak they have looked at on a map for years. A specific stretch of the GR221. A day that ends with a bottle of Mantonegro somewhere with a view. Those days do not fit a catalogue, so we build them.

## The things people ask for most

**A named summit.** Usually Massanella, the highest peak on Mallorca you can legally walk up — Puig Major, which is higher, is a military installation. Sometimes it is Puig de l'Ofre for the view, or Galatzó for the shape of it.

**A gorge day.** The Torrent de Pareis is the serious one: a descent through a limestone canyon that involves scrambling, down-climbing and, in places, swimming. It needs settled weather, a dry catchment and a group that genuinely wants it.

**Food and wine.** A walk in the morning, and a table in the afternoon — a winery in Binissalem, or a farmhouse lunch somewhere in the foothills. We carry a proper picnic if you would rather eat at the top: island cheeses, sobrassada, fruit, and something from a village bakery.

**Photography.** Which mostly means timing. The best light in the Tramuntana is the half hour either side of sunrise, and the hour before the sun goes into the sea off Deià. Both require being somewhere specific at an inconvenient time, which is the whole job.

**Several days linked together.** The GR221 Dry Stone Route runs the length of the range, and a three to five day section of it is one of the best walking weeks in the Mediterranean. We plan the stages around your group rather than around the standard refuge spacing, which usually makes it considerably more pleasant.

## Working with companies and retreats

We also work as freelance guides for travel companies, retreat organisers and hotels that need the mountain side handled properly — route planning, risk assessment, first-aid cover, and guiding on the day. If you are building a programme around walking and would rather not carry that responsibility yourself, write to us.

## How to start

Send us the idea, even if it is vague. "Something with a proper summit but my mother is 68" is a completely workable brief. We will come back with a plan, the real numbers, and a price for the whole thing.
