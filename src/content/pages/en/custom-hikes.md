We work both directly with groups and as freelance guides for travel companies and retreat organisers who need someone local handling the mountain side.

## What we take on

**Route planning** around your group rather than around the standard stages — which usually makes a programme considerably more pleasant.

**Safety and risk assessment**, with wilderness first-aid cover and communication equipment on every day out.

**Guiding on the day**, in English, Spanish, Catalan or Dutch.

**The logistics** that eat a programme organiser's time: meeting points, transport at either end of a linear route, and what to do when the forecast changes.

## Who this is for

Retreat organisers adding a walking day. Travel companies without a guide on the island. Hotels whose guests keep asking. Companies wanting a day out that is not a ropes course.

Tell us what you are building and we will tell you plainly what is realistic in the dates you have.
