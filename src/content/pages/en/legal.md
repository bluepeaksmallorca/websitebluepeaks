## Privacy

### What this website collects

**Nothing.** This site has no analytics, no advertising pixels, no embedded
social widgets, no comment system and no contact form that posts to a server.
It sets **no cookies**. The fonts are served from this domain rather than from
Google, so loading a page does not make a request to any third party.

The only way to send us information through this site is by choosing to email
or message us, using the links provided.

### What happens when you contact us

When you write to us by email or WhatsApp we receive whatever you send: usually
your name, your contact details, your dates, and what you tell us about your
group. We use it to answer you and to run the day you book, and nothing else.

We do not sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to a mailing list.

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as it is useful for an active or
likely booking, and booking records for the period Spanish tax law requires.

### Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about
you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to our processing it.
Write to hola@bluepeaksmallorca.com and we will respond within one month.

If you believe we have handled your data improperly, you can complain to the
Spanish data protection authority, the **Agencia Española de Protección de
Datos** (aepd.es).
