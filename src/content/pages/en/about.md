We live in **Alaró**, a village of about five thousand people where the plain runs into the mountains. The castle on the cliff above the village is a twenty-minute drive and an hour's walk from our front door, which is roughly how this all started.

## Why we do it this way

There is a version of guided walking on this island that involves a coach, a group of thirty, and a schedule that makes a stop feel like a problem. We wanted to build the opposite.

So Blue Peaks is two people. When you write, one of us reads it and answers it. That same person meets you at the trailhead. There is nothing in between — no booking desk, no dispatcher, no guide you have never spoken to. It is a deliberately small business, and the ceiling on how many days we can take each week is the price of that.

## What we actually bring

Anyone can follow a marked path. What we think is worth paying for is the layer underneath it:

**Reading the day.** Which route works in this wind, from this direction, at this time of year. Where the ice sits in February. Which gorge is a bad idea after rain three valleys away.

**Reading the landscape.** The Serra de Tramuntana is a UNESCO World Heritage site for its *cultural* landscape, not its scenery — the terraces, water channels, charcoal platforms and mule tracks built by hand over a thousand years. Once someone shows you how to see it, you cannot unsee it, and the walking changes completely.

**Pacing.** Most people walk the first climb too fast and pay for it all afternoon. A good part of the job is simply setting a rhythm that means you finish the day well.

## Languages

Between us we guide in **English, Spanish, Catalan, German and Croatian**. Tell us what you would prefer when you write and we will let you know which of us is taking your day.

## Safety

Both of us hold current **wilderness first-aid qualifications**, and we carry a first-aid kit and communication equipment on every day out. We also carry professional liability insurance.

That said: mountain walking has inherent risk, and no amount of preparation removes it. We ask everyone to hold their own travel insurance covering hiking, and we will tell you honestly when we think a plan is beyond what a group should attempt. Turning around is a normal outcome, not a failed day.
