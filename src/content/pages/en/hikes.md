Some people arrive knowing exactly what they want. A peak they have looked at on a map for years. A day that ends with a bottle of Mantonegro somewhere with a view. Those days do not fit a catalogue, so we build them.

## The things people ask for most

**A named summit.** Usually Massanella, the highest peak on Mallorca you can legally walk up. Sometimes Puig de l'Ofre for the view, or Galatzó for the shape of it.

**Food and wine.** A walk in the morning, a table in the afternoon — a winery in Binissalem, or a farmhouse lunch in the foothills. We carry a proper picnic if you would rather eat at the top.

**Photography.** Which mostly means timing. The best light in the Tramuntana is the half hour either side of sunrise, and the hour before the sun goes into the sea off Deià.

**Several days linked together.** A three to five day section of the GR221 Dry Stone Route is one of the best walking weeks in the Mediterranean.

## How to start

Send us the idea, even if it is vague. "Something with a proper summit but my mother is 68" is a completely workable brief.
